Our story

We make time work for you.

Smart Acteris was founded on a simple belief: the most personal device you own should help you live better, not demand more of your attention.

Mission

Helping 100 million people understand their health by 2030.

It started in 2018 in a small Lagos workshop, where two engineers and a watchmaker set out to build a wearable that was accurate, beautiful and honest about your data.

Today, Smart Acteris is a team of 600 people across four continents, but we still prototype every watch by hand and wear-test it for a thousand hours before it ships.
