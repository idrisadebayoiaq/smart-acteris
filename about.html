Our Story

From a two-bay workshop to a global partner.

SNARTEX started in 2004 with two engineers, one manual lathe and a promise: never ship a part we wouldn't put in our own machines. That standard still defines us.

Today we run 180 engineers and technicians, 60+ CNC centers and a metrology lab that verifies every critical dimension before a part leaves the floor.
